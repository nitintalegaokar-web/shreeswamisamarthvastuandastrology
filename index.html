Calculation conventions

Offline geocentric positions use Astronomy Engine 2.1.19. Sidereal positions use your worksheet's birth ayanamsha, advanced by 50.29 arcseconds per year. Rahu is the mean lunar node; Ketu is 180° opposite. Significators are selected from any of the relevant houses using the chosen method. Event house sets are editable KP conventions.
