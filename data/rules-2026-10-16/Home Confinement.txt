Home Confinement
One can free from house confinement if 2nd (Freedom) signifies 2 (Freedom)-4 (Change of environment, Home)-11 (Fulfill of desire), during the joint period of 2-4-11.
One can free from house confinement if 12th (any kind of Confinement) signifies 2 (Freedom)-4 (Change of environment, Home)-11 (Fulfill of desire), during the joint period of 2-4-11.
