Love Affair & Marriage
Love affair:
1) If 5th cusp sub lord signifies 11-2 (7), have a love partner/success in love affair (falls in love without sexual contact) during the joint period of 2-5-11.
2) If 5th cusp sublord signifies 5 & 12 or 5 & 8 or 11 & 8, having a love affair with sexual relations during period 5-8-12-11.
3) If 5th cusp sub lord signifies 5-8-12, scandalous love affair during joint period 5-8-12.
4) If 5th cusp sub lord signifies 5-6-8-12 (10), termination of love affair during joint period 5-6-8-12 (10).
5) If 5th cusp sub lord signifies 7 and 7th cusp sub lord signifies 5, then only love marriage is promised, during the joint period of 7-11-2-5.

Marriage:
If the cuspal sub lord of the 7th be the significator of 2 (Addition in family, Family life) - 7 (Marriage) - 11 (permanent relationship) marriage takes place during the joint period of 2-7-11.

Love marriage: If 5th cusp sub lord signifies 7 and 7th cusp sub lord signifies 5, then only love marriage is promised during 7-11-2-5.

Divorce/Separation:
If the sub lord of the 7th cusp be the significator of 1-6-10 (being 12th from 2-7-11), it does not promise marriage or denotes absence of married life.
If already married, during 1-6-10 joint period there may be no conjugal life or estrangement/separation; it also denotes divorce.

Reunion with spouse:
If the cuspal sub lord of the 7th be the significator of 2-7-5-11, reunion takes place during the joint period of 2-7-5-11.

Second Marriage:
If 7th cusp sub lord is Mercury or deposited in a dual sign (Gemini, Sagittarius, Pisces) or any planet occupying/deposited in star of dual sign or in the star of owner of dual sign (Mercury/Jupiter) and is a significator of 2-11, then during 2-11 there is a chance of second marriage.

 LOVE & MARRIAGE
GENERAL MARRIAGE RULES
001 Which Planets are relevant for love and marriage?
 Venus is the chief karaka of love and marriage. It also indicates love and sexual instinct of the native.
 Note: Sun is not good for married life as it indicates illegal relation with more than one partner when positioned in 7th house. The only exception is when Sun is placed in its own sign Leo.

002 Which Houses are considered for love and marriage?
The houses relevant for love and marriage are: -
 2nd House denotes family or kutumbha;
 3rd house indicates friendship and relationship;
 4th house denotes sexual pleasures between husband and wife in home;
 5th House denotes love marriage, love affairs, fulfillment of sexual pleasure and the quantum of success or failure achieved in love affairs;
 7th house denotes husband and contract of marriage in legal way;
 8th house denotes sexual enjoyment, dowry and parents·expenditure on wedding;
 9th house represents negotiation or contract for marriage as it is3rd from the 7th house;
 11th House indicates friendship, pleasure and strong ties, which may not be permanent;
 12th House indicates bed comforts and illegal affairs;
 In case where marriage is being solemnized to a foreigner, then 3rd, 9th or 12th house of the bride·s chart indicates travel to far-off place, preferably in a foreign country, for settling with groom's family; and
 6th or 10th house indicates break in love affairs.

003 Rule for Promise of Marriage:
 First Step:
x 7th Cuspal Sub Lord should signify 2nd or 7th or 11th house.
x OR 7th Cuspal Sub Lord and 2nd, 5th or 11th Cuspal Sub Lord should be the same planet.
 Alternative Step: If the aforesaid rule is not satisfied, then 7th Cuspal Sub Lord should signify 5th house or 8th house.

004 Rule for No Marriage at all/ Rule for denial of Marriage:
 First Step: If 7th Cuspal Sub Lord signifies 6th, 12th and 10th House; without having any connection to 2nd, 3rd, 7th, 11th or 5th house,then the native will never get married.
 Alternative Step: If 7th Cuspal Sub Lord does not signify 2nd, 7th or 11th house at stellar level and sub level, then the native remain unmarried forever.
Note:
x Please remember that this situation will be very rare, hence one should be careful in making this prediction.
x If a planet is significator of 2nd, 7th or 11th house and it itself happens to be a single strong significator of 6th house (denoting separation), then that planet will not allow marriage in its Dasha or Bhukti.

005 Rule for Knowing Reasons for Marriage:
Note the 7th Cuspal Sub Lord and observe the house in which it is positioned.
 If it is positioned in 1st house, then personal concerns will be the main reason for marriage;
 If it is positioned in 2nd house, then financial security or family concerns will be the main reason for marriage;
 If it is positioned in 3rd house, then agreement or contract will be the main reason for marriage;
 If it is positioned in 4th house, then mother or property will be the main reason for marriage;
 If it is positioned in 5th house, then knowledge, love or colleagues will be the main reason for marriage;
 If it is positioned in 6th house, then health problems will be the main reason for marriage;
 If it is positioned in 7th house, then ex-partner or business deals will be the main reason for marriage;
 If it is positioned in 8th house, then debts or legal issues will be the main reason for marriage;
 If it is positioned in 9th house, then father, stranger, internet friends or higher education will be the main reason for marriage;
 If it is positioned in 10th house, then job or status in society will be the main reason for marriage;
 If it is positioned in 11th house, then colleagues or known friends will be the main reason for marriage; 
 If it is positioned in 12th house, then spiritual people, foreigners or unknown people will be the main reason for marriage.

006 Rule for Delay in Marriage/ Rule for Late Marriage/ Rule for failing of Marriage proposals:
 First Step:
x 7th Cuspal Sub Lord should signify 2nd, 7th or 11th house and should be connected to 4th and 10th house also.
x Further, 7th Cuspal Sub Lord should not signify 3rd house.
 Alternative Step:
x If 7th Cuspal Sub Lord does not signify 2nd, 7th or 11th house at stellar signification, then sub level signification to 2nd, 7th or 11th house shall be studied.
x If the Sub Lord of 7th Cuspal Sub Lord signifies 2nd, 7th or 11th house, then the native will get married, but late in life.
 Note: Indication of Punarphoo Dosha, a condition of conjunction or aspect of Saturn and Moon in a horoscope, also indicates delay in marriage.

007 Rule for marriage with the same girl twice:
 7th Cuspal Sub Lord should be aspected by Lord of 8th house and there must be a Punarphoo Dosha in the houses under consideration for marriage.
 Note: This condition may arise in situation where the first marriage had occurred after running from home and then the couple subsequently got married again in front of the society after acceptance by both the families.

008 Rule for Grand Marriage:
 11th Cuspal Sub Lord should signify 11th house along with 2nd and 7th house.

009 Rule for marrying rich bride or groom:
 First Step: 7th Cuspal Sub Lord should be deposited in the constellation of a planet which signifies 2nd, 7th and 11th house.
 Second Step: The significator of 2nd, 7th and 11th house in one horoscope should also be the Ruling Planets in another·s horoscope.

010 Rule for knowing the physical feature and characteristics of spouse
The physical feature and characteristics of spouse will depend upon 7th Cuspal Sub Lord and the Sign in which it is positioned. Hence the same is to be noted.
 If it is positioned in Aries, then the person will generally be of average height and possess lean and muscular body (neither stout nor thick). He/She may be of wheatish complexion with expressive eyes, long face and curly hair.
 If it is positioned in Taurus, then the person will generally be of medium stature with broad forehead, thick neck and bright eyes. He/she may have dark hair, fair complexion, broad shoulders,
well developed muscles and will be of cheering personality.
 If it is positioned in Gemini, then the person will generally be tall and upright coupled with moderate complexion (neither too fair nor too dark). He/She may have long hands, sharp eyes, long nose and charming personality.
 If it is positioned in Cancer, then the person will generally be of short stature with heavy body, slender limbs, powerful claws and prominent abdomen. The portion above abdomen will be more prominent than the lower parts. He/She may also walk with a rolling gait/pace.
 If it is positioned in Leo, then the person will generally be of moderate stature with a muscular body, well developed bones and broad forehead. He/She may have royal outlook, dignified personality and commanding appearance.
 If it is positioned in Virgo, then the person will generally have a tall and slender body. He/She may have shrill voice and curved eyebrows with bushy hair. He/She may often appear to be younger than his/her actual age. They will be very active and brisk walking people.
 If it is positioned in Libra, then the person will generally be of tall stature with well-proportioned body having slender but strong limbs and parrot like nose. He/She may be graceful and have humanitarian instincts with charming appearance and influential personality.
 If it is positioned in Scorpio, then the person will generally be of average stature with well-proportioned body having long hands and broad face. He/She may have commanding appearance with good and respectful personality.
 If it is positioned in Sagittarius, then the person will generally be of tall stature with well-developed muscles and broad forehead.He/She may have bushy eyebrows, long nose, graceful bright eyes, smiling face, fair complexion and handsome figure.
 If it is positioned in Capricorn, then the person will generally be emaciated, gaunt and weak. He/She may have long nose, deep set eyes, and coarse hair but the body growth will be slow.
 If it is positioned in Aquarius, then the person will generally be tall having fair complexion and handsome appearance.
 If it is positioned in Pisces, then the person will generally be short heighted having plumpy body, small hands and small feet.

011 Rule for checking about the partner:
7th Cuspal Sub Lord indicates the Spouse; hence the details of the spouse can be checked by churning the results of the following steps:
 First Step: Note the 7th Cuspal Sub Lord and observe the house where it is placed.
 Second Step: Further, note the house where constellation lord of 7th Cuspal Sub Lord is placed.

012 Rule for judging the age difference between partners:
Each planet indicates certain age difference:
x Saturn indicates much difference;
x Mars, Mercury and Moon indicate very little difference;
x Jupiter, Venus and Sun indicate proper difference; and
x Rahu and Kethu indicates the age of planet which is the lord of the zodiac sign in which it is positioned.
7th Cuspal Sub Lord indicates the age differences between partners, hence 7th Cuspal Sub Lord and Constellation Lord in which the 7th Cuspal Sub Lord is positioned is to be noted and predictions is to be made as follows:
x If both the planets are Saturn, then the age difference between partners will be more than 8-9 years.
x In case, if one planet is Saturn and the other planet is Moon, then the age difference between partners will be around 5-7 years.

013 Rule for checking who will survive whom:
 Rule from Husband’s Chart: If 7th Cuspal Sub Lord is positioned in dual sign and it also happens to be a significator of Marakasthana and Badhakasthana house, then his wife will die earlier, i.e., he will survive longer than his wife.
 Rule from Wife’s Chart: If 7th Cuspal Sub Lord is positioned in dual sign and it also happens to be a significator of Marakasthana and Badhakasthana houses, then her husband will die earlier, i.e., she will survive longer than her husband.

014 Rule to determine who will dominate Husband or Wife:
 Rule from Husband’s Chart: - If 7th Cuspal Sub Lord is a significator of 7th, 8th, 12th and 5th house, then his wife will dominate over all the affairs of life.
 Rule from Wife Chart: - If 7th Cuspal Sub Lord is a significator of 7th, 8th, 12th and 5th house, then her husband will dominate over all the affairs of the life.
