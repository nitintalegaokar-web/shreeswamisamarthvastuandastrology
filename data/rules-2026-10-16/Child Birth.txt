Child Birth
If the sub lord of the 5th cusp (Female) or the 11th cusp (Male) is the significator of 2 (Increase in family) - 5 (Conception, child) - 11 (Desire), child birth is promised during joint period of 2-5-11.
If it is the significator of 1-4-10 (being 12th from 2-5-11), child birth is not promised.
If the cuspal sub lord of the 5th house occupies fruitful sign Cancer, Scorpio or Pisces and is the significator of 2-5-11, the woman concerned is pregnant.
If the cuspal sub lord of the 5th house occupies barren sign Aries, Gemini, Leo or Virgo and is in the star of the planet in the barren sign, then the woman concerned is not pregnant.
If the cuspal sub lord of the 5th house occupies the star of the planet in dual sign Gemini, Sagittarius, Pisces or Mercury, the woman delivers twins or more than one child.
