Election — Timing & Outcome
Timing:
If 3rd (Election, view of people) sub lord signifies 3 (Election) - 9 (Opposite view) - 11 (Fulfillment of desire), election will be held during the joint period of 3-9-11.

Outcome:
If Ascendant or 10th sub lord (Tendency of people of country) signifies 6 (Military dictatorship) - 11 (Gain), power comes to Military/dictator.
If Ascendant or 10th sub lord signifies 4 (Democracies, democratic government and movements) - 11 (Gain), power comes to Democratic Government.
If Ascendant or 10th sub lord signifies 9 (Religious leader) - 11 (Gain), power comes to Religious leaders.
