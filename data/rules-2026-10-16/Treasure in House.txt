Treasure in House
If the sub lord of the 4th cusp be the significator of the houses 2-11-6 and connected with Saturn, one will surely have the treasure in house during joint period of 2-6-11.
If the sub-lord of the 11th cusp be the significator of the houses 4-11, one will surely have the treasure in house during joint period of 4-8-11.
