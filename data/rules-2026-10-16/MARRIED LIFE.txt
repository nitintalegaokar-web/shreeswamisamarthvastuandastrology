MARRIED LIFE
028 Rule for Happy Married Life/ Rule for harmonious married
Life/ Rule for checking good relation between spouses:
 First Step: 11th Cuspal Sub Lord should signify 7th house or 11th
house.
 Alternative Step: 11th Cuspal Sub Lord should signify 2nd, 5th, 7th
or 11th house, without signifying 6th house and/or 12th house.
 Alternative Step: 7th Cuspal Sub Lord is either Venus or Jupiter
and should signify 2nd and 11th house.
029 Rule for Unhappy Married Life/ Rule for frequent quarrel or
displeasure in Married life:
 First Step: 11th Cuspal Sub Lord should strongly signify 6th house
(denoting deserting by the partner), 8th house (denoting
disharmony and quarrel) and 12th House (denoting native seeks
separation); without having signification to 7th or 11th house;
 Alternative Step: 7th Cuspal Sub Lord should signify 6th, 8th or 12th
house.
 Note: -
x Role of Mars: Mars in 8th house indicates quarrel and displeasure
between partners, except when Mars occupies its own sign.
x Role of Saturn: Saturn in 8th house indicates disturbed married
life and lack of warmness between the couple. It may also be on
account of denial of sexual intercourse.
INTER-CASTE / UNCONVENTIONAL MARRIAGE

and Sub Lord) should be connected to either Mars or Saturn or
Rahu.

LIVE-IN RELATIONSHIP
031 Rule for going for live-in relationship:
 First Step: 11th Cuspal Sub Lord should not be in retrograde
motion.
 Second Step: 11th Cuspal Sub Lord should not be deposited in
constellation of a planet which is retrograde in motion.
 Third Step: 11th Cuspal Sub Lord should signify 2nd, 5th, 7th and
11th house.
032 Rule for checking who will start the live-in relationship:
 Rule for native/querent: If 11th Cuspal Sub Lord signifies 3rd
house, then the querent will take the initiative for staring the
relationship.
 Rule for person other than querent: If 11th Cuspal Sub Lord
signifies 9th house, then the other person will take the initiative
for starting the relationship.
 Timing of Event: The conjoined period of significators of 2nd, 5th,
7th and 11th house will be taken into consideration for
fructification of the event which should agree with the ruling
planets at the date, place and time of judgement.
LOVE AFFAIRS
033 Rule for love marriage/ Rule for materialization of love
affair into marriage
 First Step: 7th Cuspal Sub Lord should signify 5th house and either
2nd or 7th or 11th house. Further 5th Cuspal Sub Lord should also
signify 2nd, 7th or 11th house.
 Alternative Step: 5th Cuspal Sub Lord and 7th Cuspal Sub Lord
should be the same planet.
 Alternative Step: 5th Cuspal Sub Lord should directly/indirectly be
connected to 5th house and 7th house.
 Alternative Step: 5th Cuspal Sub Lord should be strong significator
of 7th house and 11th house.Basic Rules & Applied Horoscopes

034 Rule for judging whether the love affair is one side or both
sided:
 11th Cuspal Sub Lord and 5th Cuspal Sub Lord are to be studied
for judging whether love affair is both sided or one sided.
In case the query is asked by the girl querent, then, if 5th Cuspal
Sub Lord signifies 11th house, it is predicted that the girl loves
the boy.
035 Rule for checking whether boy loves the girl:
 For knowing this, the horary chart must be rotated to 11th
house, so that the 11th house can be treated as the ascendant
of the boy.
Now in this rotated chart, if 3rd Cuspal Sub Lord (i.e., 5th from 11th
house) signifies 1st house, then it is predicted that the boy loves the
girl.
036 Rule for Time pass love/ Rule for Casual Love affair
 First Step: If 5th Cuspal Sub Lord is directly /indirectly connected
to 6th house and 12th house along with the signification of 2nd, 5th,
7th and 11th house, then it will be a time pass love affair.
 Alternative Step: If 5th Cuspal Sub Lord signifies both positive
houses (2nd, 5th, 7th and 11th house) as well as negative houses (6th
and 12th house), then it will be a casual love affair.
037 Rule for Failure /Non-Materialization of Love affair into
marriage:
 First Step: 5th Cuspal Sub Lord should be deposited in
constellation of a planet which signifies 6th house and/or 12th
house.
 Alternative Step: OR 5th Cuspal Sub Lord should be deposited in
the constellation of a planet which signifies 4th house and 10th
house.
038 Rule for judging who will drop from the idea of love
marriage:
 Rule for dropping of idea by the Querent: If 5th Cuspal Sub Lord is
deposited in constellation of a planet which signifies 12th house,
then the querent will drop the idea of love marriage.
 Rule for dropping of idea by the Lover: If 5th Cuspal Sub Lord is
deposited in constellation of a planet which signifies 6th house,
then the querent·s lover will withdraw from the idea of love
marriage.A Compendium on KP Astrology
