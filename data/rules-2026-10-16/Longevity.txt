Longevity: दीर्घायु

Rule 1:
If the cuspal sub lord of the Ascendant, be the significator of the house 6 or 8 or 12 the life is short (about 33 years).
If the cuspal sub lord of the Ascendant, be the significator of the house 1 or 5 or 9 or 10 the life is long (about more than 66 years).
If the cuspal sub lord of the Ascendant, be the significator of the house 6 or 8 or 12 and 1 or 5 or 9 or 10, the life is medium (about 66 years).
But if the cuspal sub lord of the Ascendant, be the significator of the Badhaka and Maraka houses with the above mentioned houses, it is not good for life.
If the cuspal joint rulers (the sign lord, star lord and sub lord) of the Ascendant or the 8th cusp be the significator of the Badhaka and Maraka houses, their joint period is very critical for life.
In the short life term within 33 years, in the medium life term within 66 years and in the long life term above 66 years, also check the 3rd and 8th cusps too.
Badhaka bhava is more danger than Maraka bhava. So while checking about longevity, first check the Badhaka bhava and then Maraka bhava.

Rule 2:
If the cuspal sub lord of the Ascendant be the significator of the Badhaka and Maraka houses, the life term is short during their joint period and is very critical for life.
