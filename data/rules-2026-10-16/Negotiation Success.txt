Negotiation Success
If the sub-lord of the 3rd cusp be the significator of the houses 6 and 11, then one will have success. But if it be significator of the houses 5 and 12, one cannot have success.
The houses which are to be considered for judgment depend on query: marriage (7th, 3, 9); business/job (10th, 6, 3, 9); adoption (5th, 3, 9).
