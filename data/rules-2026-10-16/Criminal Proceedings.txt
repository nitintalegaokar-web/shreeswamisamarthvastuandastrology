Criminal Proceedings (आपराधिक कार्यवाही)
If the sub lord of the 10th cusp (status) be the significator of 7 (court proceedings) - 8 (infamy) - 12 (crimes), the native will be involved in court proceedings relating to crimes, be ill-reputed and punished during the joint period of 7-8-12.
If the sub lord of the 12th cusp (jail) be the significator of 3 (away from home), 8 (misfortune) or 12 (restrictions), one has to court jail during the joint period of 3-8-12.
