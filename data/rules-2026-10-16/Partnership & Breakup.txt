Partnership & Breakup
If the sub lord of the 8th cusp (partner’s money) be the significator of 6-11, the native gets a partner.
If the sub lord of the 8th cusp (partner’s money) be the significator of 5-12, the native will not get a partner.
If the sub lord of the 7th cusp (partnership) be the significator of 5-11, the partnership continues for a long time.
If the sub lord of the 7th cusp be the significator of 8-12, the partnership will break.
If 7th cusp sub lord be the significator of 6th, partner will break; if significator of 12th, querent will break partnership during joint period of 6-12.
If owner of 7th and occupant of 7th be a significator of 11th, querent gets benefit from partnership. But if they be a significator of 7-12, it is loss in partnership.
