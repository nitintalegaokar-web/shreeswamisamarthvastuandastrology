DEBAUCHERY AND INCEST

and Sub Lord) shall be connected fully to either Saturn or Mars
or Venus.
 Second Step: All 5th Cusp Co-Rulers (Sign Lord, Constellation Lord
and Sub Lord) shall be connected fully to either Saturn or Mars
or Venus.
 Third Step: All 11th Cusp Co-Rulers (Sign Lord, Constellation Lord
and Sub Lord) shall be connected fully to either Saturn or Mars
or Venus.
Note: For the benefit of readers, it is concluded that if all the co rulers
of 1st house, 5th house and 11th house are connected to Saturn or Mars
or Venus; then the person will be debauch, even to the extent of
committing incest (having sexual pleasure prior to legal marriage).
