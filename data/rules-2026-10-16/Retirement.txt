Retirement in Job
For job/business we check 2-6-10. 12th from these (1-5-9) are negative for 2-6-10. Thus for retirement consider 1-5-9-12.
If sub lord of 10th or 6th be the significator of 1-5-9-12, the querent can retire from service during the joint period of 1-5-9-12. (Generally connected with 2-6-10 service houses.)
