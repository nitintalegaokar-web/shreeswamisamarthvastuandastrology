Desired Job / Promotion
Desired job:
If the cuspal sub lord of the 6th or 10th be the significator of 2-6-10, service or earning is promised during 2-6-10. If the cuspal sub lord of the 11th be the significator of 2-6-10-11, the querent gets a job of expectation during 2-6-10.
Promotion:
If the cuspal sub lord of the 11th be the significator of 2-6-10-11, promotion in service is promised during 2-6-10-11.
