Accident & Assassination
Accident / escape from accident:
If the sub lord of the Ascendant, 8th or 12th cusp be the significator of 8 or 12, the native is likely to meet with an accident.
If the sub lord of the Ascendant, 8th or 12th cusp be the significator of 8 or 7 or 2, the native is likely to meet with a fatal accident; if significator of 1 or 5 or 11, one will be safe from accidents.
If the sub lord of the 8th or 12th cusp be the significator of 1-5-11 the native will overcome danger, during the joint period of 1-5-11.
Accidents and unnatural deaths are caused during the joint period of the significators of 8-12, Badhaka and Maraka.

Assassinations (हत्या):
If 8th or 12th sub lord signifies 8-12-Badhaka-Maraka, one can get assassinated in the joint period of 8-12-Badhaka-Maraka.
If 8th or 12th sub lord signifies 8-12-Badhaka-Maraka, the native is likely to meet with a fatal assassination during joint period of 8-12-Maraka and Badhaka. If it be a significator of 1-5-11, one will be safe from assassination.
