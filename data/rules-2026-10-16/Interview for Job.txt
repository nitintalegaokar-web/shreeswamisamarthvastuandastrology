Interview for Job
3rd indicates interview/meeting; 7th indicates person we meet; from 7th, 3rd is 9th — so meeting with other person is 9th cusp. For interview consider 3rd and 9th cusps. For fulfillment, consider 11th cusp.
If the cuspal sub lord of the 11th be the significator of 3 (Meeting, Interview) - 9 (meeting with person) - 11 (Gain, Success, fulfillment), a meeting is possible.
If the cuspal sub lord of the 3rd or 9th be the significator of 2-6-10-11 then meeting for job is successful during the joint period of the significators of 2-6-10-11.
