Imprisonment / Bail
Imprisonment:
If the cuspal sub lord of the 12th be the significator of 3 (away from home) - 8 (misfortune) - 12 (restrictions), and connected with Mars or Saturn or Rahu or Ketu, one has to face jail during the joint period of 3-8-12.

Bail:
One can get bail if 12th (any kind of confinement) signifies 2 (Freedom) - 4 (change of environment, Home) - 11 (Fulfillment of desire) during the joint period of 2-4-11.
