Arrest & Legal Proceedings
Arrest a person:
If 6th sub lord (Police) signifies 1 (Self effort) and 11 (Success), Police will be able to arrest runaway person during the joint period of 1-11.
If 11th sub lord (Court order) signifies 6 (Victory on enemy) - 11 (Success), one can get arrested by police on court order during the joint period of 6-11.
If 7th sub lord signifies 5 (Gain to opponent) - 10 (4th from 7th come back home) - 6 (12th from 7th Lost reputation), arrest on court order during joint period of 5-6-10.
