Gender of Child
Sun, Mars, Jupiter are male planets; Moon, Venus, and Rahu are female; Mercury and Ketu are neuter. Odd signs (1-3-5-7-9-11) indicate male and even signs (2-4-6-8-10-12) indicate female.
5th cusp sign-star-sub lord indicates gender of child. (Prediction is difficult and must be judged holistically.)
