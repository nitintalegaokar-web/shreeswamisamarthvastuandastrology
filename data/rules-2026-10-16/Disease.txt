Disease (6th)
If the sub lord of the 6th cusp be the significator of 6-8-12 and connected with 1, the native may suffer from disease during the sub period of this cuspal sub lord of 6.
If the sub lord of the 8th or 12th cusp be the significator of 6-8-12 then native suffers from the disease and permanent defect.
During the joint period of the significators of 1-5-11 disease is cured, during the joint period of the significator of 2-11 relieved from hospital.
