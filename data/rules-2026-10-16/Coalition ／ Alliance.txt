Coalition / Alliance (and Breaking)
If the cuspal sub lord of the 11th (Coalition) be the significator of 4 (support/supporter) - 7 (Alliance) - 11, possibility of coalition/alliance between political party/company during joint period of 4-7-11.
Breaking of Coalition/Alliance: If 11th cuspal sub lord be the significator of 12 (unfortunate circumstances) - 8 (Breaking up) - 1 (Interruption), breaking is possible during 1-8-12.
