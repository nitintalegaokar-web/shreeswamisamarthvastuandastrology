Independent Business
If the cuspal sub lord of the 10th (Independent business) be the significator of 2 (Money/wealth) - 7 (Business with others) - 10 (Business), an independent business or profession is promised during the joint period of 2-7-10.
If the cuspal sub lord of the 7th be the significator of 2-10-11, gain in business is promised during the joint period of 2-10-11.
