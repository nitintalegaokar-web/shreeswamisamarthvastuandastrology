Missing Person / Pet
Missing person:
If the cuspal sublord of 11th be the significator of 2-8-11, a person returns home during the joint period of 2-8-11.

Missing Pet:
If the cuspal sublord of 11th be the significator of 2, 8 or 11, a pet returns home during the joint period of 2, 8 and 11. (Also check 4th Sub Lord as 6th is pet and 11th from 6th is 4th.)
