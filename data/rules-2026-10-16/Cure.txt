Cure (1,5,11)
If the sublord of the 6th or 8th or 12th or 11th cusp be the significator of 1-5-11 disease is cured, or the native is saved from any danger.
During the joint period of the significators of 1-5-11, disease is cured or the native is saved from any danger.
Astrologers need to select from sublord of Ascendant or 6th or 8th or 12th as per their experience. It is advisable that first examine all the cusp sub lords and then come to any conclusion.
If the sub lord of the Ascendant cusp be the significator of 1-5-11, the native may recover after accident or escape from accident during the sub period of this cuspal sublord of 1-5-11. (In Char lagna consider 9th instead of 11).
During the joint period of the significator of 2-4-11 discharge from hospital.
