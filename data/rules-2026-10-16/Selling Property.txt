Selling Property (House/Land)
If the cuspal sub lord of the 10th house (land/building of opponent, 4th from 7th) be the significator of 3 (absence of land/building, 12th from 4th) - 5 (success/fulfillment of desire of opponent, 11th from 7th) - 10 (land/building of opponent, 4th from 7th), selling is promised during the joint period of 3-5-10.
