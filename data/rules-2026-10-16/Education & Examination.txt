Education & Examination
Education:
If the sub lord of the 4th cusp be the significator of 4 (education, preparations for examinations, academic qualifications) - 9 (deep study and higher education) - 11 (success, fulfillment), the native will get academic qualifications during the joint period of 4-9-11.
Education will either be completed or postponed during the joint period of the significators of 3-8-10 (being 12th from 4-9-11 respectively).

Success in examination:
If the sub lord of the 4th cusp is the significator of 4 (examinations) - 9 (higher studies) - 11 (Success), success in examination is promised, when during the days of examination the joint period of 4-9-11 is active.
