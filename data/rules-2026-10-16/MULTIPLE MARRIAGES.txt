MULTIPLE MARRIAGES
027 Rule for Multiple Marriages:
 First Step: 7th Cuspal Sub Lord should signify 9th house along with
either 2nd or 7th or 11th house.
 Second Step: 7th Cuspal Sub Lord should be Mercury or Jupiter
OR if 7th Cuspal Sub Lord is a planet other that Mercury or
Jupiter, then it should be placed in a dual sign (Gemini, Virgo,
Sagittarius or Pisces) OR 7th Cuspal Sub Lord should be deposited
in the constellation of a planet which is in dual sign (Gemini,
Virgo, Sagittarius or Pisces).
 Third Step: 2nd Cuspal Sub Lord should signify either7th house or
11th house.
 Timing of Event: The timing for fructification of event shall be
determined by considering the planet signification of 2nd house,
7th house, 9th house and 11th house as agreeing with the ruling
planets at the time and place of judgement.
 Note: Jupiter indicates two marriages whereas Mercury indicates
multiple marriages.
