Change in Job / Change in place of Job
If the cuspal sub lord of 3 (journey; 3rd is 12th from 4th) or 12 (Life in completely unfamiliar atmosphere) be the significator of 3-12 and connected with 10, transfer from present place to another is promised.
If the sub lord of the 10th cusp be the significator of 3-12, the transfer may happen in service from one place to another.
The transfer takes place during the joint period of the significators of 3-10-12.
