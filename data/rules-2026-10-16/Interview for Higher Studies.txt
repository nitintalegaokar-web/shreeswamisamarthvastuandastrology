Interview for Higher Studies

If the cuspal sub lord of the 11th be the significator of 3 - 9 - 11, a meeting is possible.
Meeting is for higher study, so check 9th cusp sub lord. If 9th cuspal sub lord be significator of 4-9-11 then meeting for admission is successful during 4-9-11.
