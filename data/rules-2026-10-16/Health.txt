Health (1st)
If the sub lord of the Ascendant, be in the star of the occupant of the house 1 or 11, health is normal.
If the sub lord of the Ascendant, be the significator of the house 6 (sickness) or 8 (danger to life) or 12 (hospitalization), it is not good for health.
The native may suffer from sickness or disease during the joint period of the significators of 1 and 6, or 1 and 8, or 1 and 12.
