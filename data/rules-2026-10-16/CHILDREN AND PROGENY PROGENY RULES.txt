CHILDREN AND PROGENY PROGENY RULES
040 Which Houses are relevant for Child Birth?
 2nd house indicates addition of a member to family.
 5th house is the primary house of child birth.
 8th house indicates surgical implications in child birth.
 11th house indicates fulfillment of desire.
 12th house indicates hospitalization, expenditures and
misfortunes related to child birth.
041 Which Planets are relevant for Child Birth?
 Jupiter is the Chief Karaka for child.
 Mars or Sun connection indicates surgical aid.
 Kethu connection indicates abortion or miscarriage.
 Moon connection indicates emotional instability and depression.
 Saturn connection indicates delay in child birth or lapses unnoticed by doctors.
042 Which Houses represents child in a horoscope?
 5th house indicates first child;
 7th house indicates second child;
 9th house indicates third child; and
 11th house indicates fourth child and so on.
043 Rule for determining whether Child Birth is Promised or Not
 First Step: 5th Cuspal Sub Lord should be in direct motion and not
in retrograde motion.
 Second Step: 5th Cuspal Sub Lord should be deposited in the
constellation of a planet which should be in direct motion.
 Third Step: 5th Cuspal Sub Lord should signify 2nd, 5th or 11th
house.

045 How to determine the Auspicious time for conceiving for
those who have not been able to conceive?
This situation is applicable for those women who have not been able
to conceive a child since long. These women should only conceive
when the following conditions are satisfied:
 Moon should be transiting in the constellation and sub of a planet
which signifies 2nd, 5th and 11th house; without having
signification to 1st, 4th and 10th house.
 The Sub Lord of Moon should be transited by an Ascendant whose
Lord which signifies 2nd, 5th and 11th house.
 Further, the current Dasha and Bhukti should also signify 2nd, 5th
and 11th house; without having signification to 1st, 4th and 10th
house.
