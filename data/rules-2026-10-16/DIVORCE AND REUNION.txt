 First Step:
x If 7th Cuspal Sub Lord signifies 1st, 6th and 12th house along with
11th house, then marriage will take place but will end in divorce;
x OR If 7th Cuspal Sub Lord is connected to 6th, 8th and 12th house,

 Alternative Step: Promise of Second Marriage in Chart also denotes

during the conjoined period of joint significators of 1st, 6th, 10th
and 12th house as agreeing with the Ruling Planets at the date,
time and place of judgement.

main reason for divorce/separation;

 If it is positioned in 3rd house, then agreement or contract will be
the main reason for divorce/separation;
 If it is positioned in 4th house, then mother or property will be the
main reason for divorce/separation;
 If it is positioned in 5th house, then children or colleagues will be
the main reason for divorce/separation;
 If it is positioned in 6th house, then health problems will be the
main reason for divorce/separation;
 If it is positioned in 7th house, then ex-partner or business deals

 If it is positioned in 9th house, then father, stranger, internet

divorce/separation;
 If it is positioned in 10th house, then job or status in society will

 First Step: 11th Cuspal Sub Lord should signify 2nd house and 7th
house.
 Second Step: 7th Cuspal Sub Lord should be deposited in the
