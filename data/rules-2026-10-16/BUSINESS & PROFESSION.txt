BUSINESS & PROFESSION
PROFESSION INDICATED BY PLANETS
046 Professional Qualities indicated by the Planets:
 Sun indicates independent spirit and working within social
and government laws;
 Moon indicates emotional involvement for social purpose;
 Mars indicates analytical ability, argumentative disposition,
independent outlook and logical analysis;
 Mercury indicates quick speech, calculation and grasp of
arguments;
 Jupiter indicates strong knowledge and acumen to judge;
 Venus indicates managing capability without having
emotional involvement;
 Saturn indicates rendering justice, working hard and being
cool head;
 Rahu indicates working on software and big machines; and
 Kethu indicates perfectness and calculative ability.
DOING A BUSINESS
047 Rule for business or Service:
 If 10th Cuspal Sub Lord is deposited in constellation of a planet
which signifies 6th house, then it indicates that the native will
work in service sector.
 If 10th Cuspal Sub Lord is deposited in constellation of a planet
which signifies 7th house, then it indicates that the native will
carry his own business or profession.
 If 10th Cuspal Sub Lord is deposited in constellation of a planet
which signifies both 6th as well as 7th house, then it indicates that
the native will be working in service sector for some time and then
he will be switching over to his own business or profession.
