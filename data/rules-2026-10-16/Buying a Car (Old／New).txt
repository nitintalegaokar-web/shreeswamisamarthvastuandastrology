Buying a Car (Old/New)
Buying an old car:
If the cuspal sub lord of the 4th (Car) be the significator of 4 (Car) - 11 (Success/Fulfillment of desire) - 12 (Investment) one will have his own vehicle during the joint period of 4-11-12.
Note: For old car, check 10th (Old things). Benefit can be judged by 11th sub lord as well.

Buying a car (new):
If the cuspal sub lord of the 4th (Car) be the significator of 4 - 11 - 12, purchase is promised during joint period of 4-11-12.
