Job Change & Promotion
Change of job:
For job/business we check 2-6-10. 12th from these (1-5-9) are negative for 2-6-10. For change consider 1-5-9-12. If sub lord of 10th or 6th be significator of 1-5-9-12, change of job during 1-5-9; service promised when significator of 2-6-10 is active.
Promotion:
If the cuspal sub lord of the 11th be the significator of 2-6-10-11, promotion in service is promised during the joint period of 2-6-10-11.
