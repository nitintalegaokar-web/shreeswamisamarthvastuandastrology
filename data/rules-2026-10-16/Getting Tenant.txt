Getting Tenant (Vacant tenant)
Neighbour is 3rd. Tenant is 6th, thus neighbour of tenant is 8th (6th from 3rd).
If the cuspal sub lord of the 8th be the significator of 10 (3rd from 8th, loss of home), 7 (12th from 8th, loss to tenant) or 1 (11th from 3rd, gain to neighbour) vacating the house is promised during the joint period of 10-7-1.
