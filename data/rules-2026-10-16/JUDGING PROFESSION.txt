JUDGING PROFESSION
054 Rules for judging the profession of a native or querent
 Note the 10th house sign lord, star lord and sub lord.
 Note the planets occupying 10th house.
 Note the planets conjoined with 10th house.
 All the planets together shall be churned for making fruitful
prediction.
For Example:
If 10th House sign lord, star lord and sub lord planets are Sun, Mars
and Mercury, then we can state that the native/querent may have the
following professions:
x Mars represents surgical, Mercury gives quick understanding and
grasp of knowledge and Sun represents healing, doctor, etc.
Hence, the profession may be Doctor or Surgeon.
x Similarly, Mars and Mercury represent Mechanical Engineer and
Sun for government service. Hence, the profession may be
mechanical engineer in government organizations.
x Or Mars represents industrial workers, Mercury represents
accounts, audit, finance and Sun represents government service.
Hence, the native may be handling finance jobs at government
industries relating to industrial workers.
