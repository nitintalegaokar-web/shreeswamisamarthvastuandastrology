War – Timing & Outcome
Is there any war? When?
If 3rd (relations with neighbouring country) sub lord be the significator of 4th (concern of national security), 9th (foreign relation), 7th (International disputes), there will be a war during joint period of 4-9-7.

Who will win?
If 10th (national reputation) sub lord be the significator of 4 (outcome of anything) - 10 (national reputation, the end of war) - 2 (enemy’s death), one will win in war during joint period of 4-10-2.
