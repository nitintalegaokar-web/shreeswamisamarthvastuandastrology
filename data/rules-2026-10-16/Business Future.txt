Business Future
If the cuspal sub lord of the 10 (Independent business) be the significator of 2 (Money/wealth) - 7 (business with others) - 10 (Business) an independent business or profession is promised during the joint period of 2-7-10.
If the cuspal sub lord of the 7th be the significator of 2-10-11, gain in business is promised during the joint period of 2-10-11.
If the cuspal sub lord of the 7th be the significator of 7-11, partnership is promised during the joint period of 7-11.
If the cuspal sub lord of the 7th be the significator of 8-12, break-up in partnership is indicated during the joint period of 8-12.
