End of Dictator / Military Ruler
If 8th Sublord (Secret work of enemy/opposition) signifies 5 (12th from 6 — Dictator), 9 (12th from 10 — those in power), 12 (loss), ruler faces trouble and end of his govt. or rule.
If 10th (Ruler) signifies 2 (Maraka) - 7 (Maraka) - 12 (Moksha) and Badhaka, rule will end with death (2-7-12 from 10th).
